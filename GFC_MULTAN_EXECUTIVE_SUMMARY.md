# GFC Multan App - Executive Summary
## Quick Overview for Management

---

## 🎯 What We're Building

A mobile app to control product distribution, prevent fraud, manage warranties, and streamline dealer/FSM operations.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline: 14 Weeks (3.5 Months)

```
Week 1:   Planning & Design
Week 2-3:   Setup & Foundation  
Week 4-6:   Core Features (FSM, Sales Manager, Dealer)
Week 7: Advanced Features (Warehouse, Reports)
Week 8: Testing & Refinement
Week 9:    Launch & Training
```

---

## 📱 Key Features

| Module | Users | Main Function |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **FSM Module** | Field Sales Managers | Scan products, enter customer info, submit claims |
| **Sales Manager** | Sales Managers | Approve claims, activate warranties |
| **Dealer Module** | Dealers | View inventory, submit damage claims |
| **Warehouse/QC** | Warehouse Staff | Review and approve damage claims |
| **Service Center** | Service Staff | Handle repair/replacement |
