# GFC Multan App Development Plan
## Project Execution Roadmap

**Prepared for:** GFC Multan Management  
**Prepared by:** Development Team  
**Date:** December 2024

---

## 📋 Overview

This document outlines how we will build your GFC Multan mobile application to manage product distribution, warranty activation, dealer incentives, and damage claims. The process is divided into clear phases with regular checkpoints to ensure we deliver exactly what you need.

---

## 🎯 What We're Building

A mobile application that will help you:
- ✅ Track product sales and prevent unauthorized wholesale
- ✅ Control warranty activation through proper approval process
- ✅ Manage FSM incentive claims efficiently
- ✅ Handle damage claims with proper documentation
- ✅ Build a customer database for future marketing
- ✅ Reduce fraud and improve transparency

---

## 📱 App Features Summary

### For Field Sales Managers (FSM)
- Scan product barcode
- Enter customer information
- Submit incentive claims
- View claim status

### For Sales Managers
- Review and approve/reject FSM claims
- Activate warranties
- Track all sales activities
- View reports and analytics

### For Dealers
- View inventory
- Submit damage claims with photos
- Track FSM activities
- View sales reports

### For Warehouse/QC Staff
- Review damage claims
- Approve or reject claims
- Track product dispatch

### For Service Centers
- View assigned damage claims
- Update repair status
- Complete claim processing

## 🔧 Technical Details (Simplified)

### **App Platform:**
- **Android App:** Works on Android phones and tablets
- **Minimum Android Version:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
- **Internet Required:** Yes (for real-time data sync)

### **Data Storage:**
- **Cloud Database:** All data stored securely in cloud
- **Image Storage:** Product and damage claim images stored in cloud
- **Backup:** Automatic daily backups

### **Security:**
- **User Authentication:** Secure login system
- **Role-Based Access:** Each user sees only what they're allowed
- **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted securely
- **Audit Logs:** All actions are logged for security
